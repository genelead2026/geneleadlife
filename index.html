About Us

At GeneLead Life Sciences Ltd, our growth roadmap is built on commercial stability and technical excellence. We operate a highly successful dual-phase business model: supplying the immediate needs of global diagnostic laboratories with high-quality consumables while simultaneously channeling commercial momentum into the Research & Development (R&D) of advanced diagnostic assets and products. Guided by expertise in molecular biology, protein engineering, and nanotechnology, we are designing diagnostic assets engineered for seamless technology transfer and absolute compliance across MHRA-GMP, ISO 13485, and CE-IVD frameworks. Our strategic foundation is built on the principle that commercial revenue must fuel technical innovation. Under the technical advisory of experts, we are currently developing proprietary lateral flow and molecular diagnostic platforms designed for global health applications. From multiplex assay optimisation to automated extraction prototypes, we are building a portfolio of diagnostic solutions that bridge the gap between emerging research and commercial deployment. Every product we develop reflects our commitment to quality, safety, and exceptional performance.
